## 🚀 Featured case study

### InstaHelp: Fixing Reliability in 15-Minute Home Services
**[Read the full case study →](https://github.com/Rhea-1212/instahelp-prd-case-study)** · **[PRD (PDF)](https://github.com/Rhea-1212/instahelp-prd-case-study/blob/main/PRD.pdf)**

| | |
|---|---|
| **Problem** | Urban Company's InstaHelp loses **₹447 per order** (₹9 Cr revenue vs ₹119 Cr loss, Q4 FY26). The cause is unreliable fulfilment (last-minute partner cancellations, a dead-end chatbot loop, and bookings marked "complete" with no service), which undermines its 30–40% price premium. |
| **My approach** | Defined goals and non-goals, a North Star metric (Contribution Margin per Order) with L1 and counter-metrics, built two personas and a journey map, and prioritised solutions with RICE. |
| **Recommendation** | **P0: AI Real-Time Recovery.** Auto-rematch a booking when a professional cancels, or hand off to a human, so customers never hit a dead end. |
| **Key requirements** | Recovery attempt within **30 seconds** for 95% of cancellations · **≥80%** of eligible bookings recovered · customer needs **≤2 taps** to accept or decline · fully auditable event log |

*Independent case study, not affiliated with Urban Company.*
